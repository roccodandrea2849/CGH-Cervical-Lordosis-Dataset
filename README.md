# CGH-Cervical-Lordosis-Dataset
De-identified clinical dataset (n = 108) of C2–C7 absolute rotational angle (ARA), age, sex, and cervicogenic headache intensity and frequency, from Dandrea Chiropractic.
## Contents
- Dandrea Chiropractic Practice Public Dataset - Sheet1.csv: one row per patient (n = 108).
- images: six de-identified lateral cervical radiographs (Radiograph 1.jpeg, Radiograph 2.jpeg, Radiograph 3.jpeg, Radiograph 4.jpeg, Radiograph 5.jpeg, Radiograph 6.jpeg) used as examples in the related publication.
- LICENSE: CC0 1.0 Universal.

## Variables
|[age column]| Patient age at the time of the radiograph | years |
|[sex column]| Patient sex | [M / F] |
|[ARA column]| C2–C7 absolute rotational angle, a measure of cervical lordosis. Values closer to zero or positive indicate a greater loss of lordosis. | degrees |
|[intensity column]| CGH intensity | 0–10 scale |
|[frequency column]| CGH frequency | incidents per week |

## How the data were collected
- **Patients.** Patients were diagnosed with active CGH according to the Cervicogenic Headache International Study Group (CHISG) criteria. Patients under 18, pregnant patients, and patients with a history of cervical spine surgery were excluded. The 108 patients were randomly sampled from the practice's larger set of patients.
- **Dates.** All data were recorded between May 20, 2022 and January 1, 2026.
- **Radiographs.** Lateral cervical radiographs were taken as part of routine clinical care with a digital radiography system at a source-to-image distance of 183 cm (72 in) with the central ray at C4. Patients stood facing sideways to the detector with the eyes level, arms at the sides, and the head and neck in a neutral position.
- **Measurement.** C2–C7 ARA was measured in PostureRay software using the Harrison posterior tangent method. The landmarks were placed once, by hand, by R.M.D. as part of routine care.
- **Headache measures.** Intensity and frequency were recorded by the practice during diagnosis 
- **De-identification.** Names, date of birth, and any other identifying information was removed
